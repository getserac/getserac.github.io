Privacy policy

Serac

Simple, guided Activities that lower the risks to your accounts, devices, and personal information. No tech experience required.

Get help

Questions, a step that doesn't match your screen, or a link that no longer works? Email getserac@gmail.com. Tell us which Activity and step, and your iPhone's iOS version (Settings › General › About).

Please never send passwords, sign-in codes, or account details. Serac never needs them.

Common questions

Does Serac check my settings?
No. iPhone apps can't see other apps' or Apple's settings. You mark each Activity as done, so your Defense score is an estimate based on what you've marked, not a guarantee.

Where is my progress stored?
Only on your iPhone. Serac has no account and no servers. If you back up your iPhone, your progress is included in that backup.

How do I start over?
Profile › Settings › Reset all progress. You can replay the app tour from the same screen.

How do I turn reminders on or off?
Profile › Settings. Serac sends at most one reminder a day.

Will this make me completely safe?
No app can. Each Activity lowers a specific risk.
