Privacy policy

Serac app and website · Effective November 7, 2026

The short version

The Serac app collects no data about you. It has no account, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your iPhone. This website has no analytics or tracking either. Its host, GitHub, may log visitors' IP addresses under its own privacy policy. We see only emails you send us.

What the app stores, and where

To work, Serac saves your progress on your iPhone: which Activities you've marked done and when, your Defense score and Level, your place in a walkthrough, your reminder choices, which computer and browser you use (so steps match your setup), and display settings such as light or dark mode.

This information never leaves your iPhone through Serac. The app makes no internet connections and contains no third-party code. Serac never asks for your passwords, sign-in codes, or account details.

Backups

Like any app's data, your Serac progress is included in your iPhone's own backup if you use one (iCloud or a computer). Those backups are handled by Apple or by you, under your own settings. Serac has no access to them.

Links to websites

Some steps include a link to an official web page, such as a credit bureau or an account's security page. Tapping one opens it in your web browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies; Serac sends them nothing.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, Serac schedules them on your iPhone. They are not sent from a server.

Information Apple may share

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give the developer crash reports and usage statistics that don't identify you. That sharing is controlled by Apple and by you, not by Serac.

Deleting your data

Profile › Settings › Reset all progress clears your progress. Deleting the app deletes all of its data from your iPhone.

If you email us

If you email us, for help or to ask to hear about the launch or the early test, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We keep it in our Gmail inbox (a Google service) and use it only to reply, or to send the notice you asked for. We never sell it or share it with anyone else. We keep it until you ask us to delete it, which you can do any time at the address below.

Children

The Serac app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: getserac@gmail.com.
